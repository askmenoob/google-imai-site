Perkhidmatan bebas / Independent service

IMAI menerima permohonan bantuan Business Profile dan pengurusan Google Ads. IMAI bukan Google dan tidak menjamin kelulusan, kedudukan teratas atau hasil jualan. Google Business Profile sendiri adalah percuma; caj IMAI ialah caj kerja persediaan dan pengurusan yang dipaparkan dalam sebutharga.

IMAI is an independent provider of Business Profile assistance and Google Ads management. Approval, top placement and sales are not guaranteed. Business Profile itself is free. IMAI charges separately for agreed setup and management work.

Maklumat permohonan / Application information

Gmail yang disahkan, nama, hubungan perniagaan, pautan awam, lokasi, bajet, sebutharga, persetujuan, nota dan sejarah tindakan disimpan dalam storan Google Cloud persendirian untuk mengurus permohonan. Pemohon hanya boleh melihat rekod sendiri; super admin mengurus permohonan pelanggan.

Verified Gmail, applicant name, business contact details, public links, target area, budget, quotations, approvals, notes and action history are held in private Google Cloud storage to manage applications. Applicants can access their own records; the super admin manages client records.

Login, akses dan bayaran / Sign-in, access and payment

Login Google hanya menggunakan identiti asas. Ia tidak memberikan akses kepada Gmail, Google Ads, Business Profile atau kad pembayaran. Akses pengurusan diberikan melalui jemputan Google selepas persetujuan berasingan. Jangan masukkan kata laluan, OTP, nombor kad atau dokumen pengenalan.

Google sign-in uses basic identity only. It does not grant access to Gmail, Google Ads, Business Profile or payment cards. Management access uses Google invitations after separate approval. Do not submit passwords, OTPs, card details or identity documents.

Permohonan dan kelulusan sebutharga tidak mengambil bayaran atau mengaktifkan iklan. Bajet iklan dibayar terus kepada Google melalui akaun pelanggan; caj IMAI berasingan. Pengaktifan dilakukan selepas kelulusan pelanggan dan persediaan Google. Portal ini belum menyediakan pembayaran atau penerbitan iklan automatik.

Applications and quote approval do not take payment or activate ads. Ad spend is paid directly to Google through the client account; IMAI fees are separate. Activation follows client approval and Google setup. This portal does not provide automatic payments or ad publishing.

Kawalan maklumat / Information control

Pelanggan kekal mengawal akaun Google dan boleh meminta akses agensi dibuang. Hubungi IMAI untuk pembetulan, permintaan pemadaman atau pertanyaan. Rekod audit dan salinan versi boleh kekal sebelum tamat tempoh simpanan berkaitan.

Clients retain control of their Google accounts and may request agency access removal. Contact IMAI for corrections, deletion requests or enquiries. Audit and version copies may remain until applicable retention expires.
